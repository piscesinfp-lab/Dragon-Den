Chapter 01 · Bedroom + Bathroom

Paint Plan

Three colors. Two rooms. One system. Paint empty rooms before furniture arrives — you'll never want to do it the other way.

Tricorn Black
SW 6258 · Eggshell · 3 bedroom walls
Warmest near-black SW makes. Doesn't go blue or cold against honey wood floors. Gray primer first, two coats paint.
Shade-Grown
SW 6188 · Eggshell · North wall only
Deep forest green with brown undertones. Mirrors the trees outside. Paint this wall first — see how it reads before committing the rest.
Mineral Deposit
SW 9670 · Satin · All bathroom walls
Stone, water, and forest in one color. Satin finish for moisture resistance. Cure 7 full days before getting walls wet.
Ceiling — No Change
Existing cream · Both rooms
Dark walls + pale ceiling = sky through the forest canopy. The A-frame height reads bigger. Beams glow against it. Never paint the ceiling dark.
Paint Order

Monday: buy paint + prime. Tuesday: Shade-Grown north wall first, then Tricorn Black three walls. Wednesday: bathroom Mineral Deposit + hardware swap. SW Emerald Interior is worth the cost — two coats covers, three with cheaper paint. Move ALL furniture before starting.

Chapter 03 · Architecture

A-Frame Strategy

The sloped ceiling and exposed beams are your biggest design assets. No apartment has this. Use them completely.

📐

Slope Rules

  • Murphy bed on flat south wall ONLY — never on sloped walls
  • Keep tall furniture away from the low slope edges
  • Low-profile items under the slopes: floor cushion, bench, plants
  • Center zone stays clear — let the peak height breathe
  • Tricorn Black paint on slope walls down to the floor — creates a forest-cave cocoon
✨

Beam Lighting

  • Govee RGBIC strips staple-gunned to underside of every visible beam
  • Warm amber default — looks like firelight on wood grain
  • Dragon red mode: the room becomes a torch-lit lair from above
  • Ridge beam: one pendant hanging 3–4ft from a cup hook screwed directly in
  • Cord tacked along beam to outlet with small clips every 12"
🪵

Wood Harmony Formula

  • Honey floor + dark espresso furniture + forest green/charcoal textiles = the formula
  • All new furniture should be darker than the floor — never lighter
  • Beams stay natural — tape them off when painting, never cover them
  • Matte black metal bridges dark wood furniture to the warm floor tone
  • Velvet and textured fabric in forest green reads luxe against warm wood
🌿

Hanging From Beams

  • Cup hooks screwed directly into beams — structural wood, rated 20–30lbs each
  • Pre-drill 1/16" pilot hole to avoid splitting
  • Pendant: center ridge beam, cord runs along beam to outlet
  • Trailing plant hanger near the window — reads like forest vines
  • Galaxy projector aimed at slope from desk shelf corner

Chapter 05 · Desk Zone

Gaming Setup

Three monitors · Full tower · Streaming · WFH · Future VR. Everything has a place, nothing is visible that shouldn't be.

Monitor Layout

AOC 1440p dead center — primary gaming, streaming, work. Left VA2247: Discord + OBS dashboard. Right VA2247: browser + Spotify. Triple monitor arm (VIVO STAND-V003T) clamps to desk edge. Back to window — natural light from the side, no glare on screens.

🖥️

Triple Monitor Arm

  • VIVO STAND-V003T — handles mixed sizes, full articulation per screen
  • Clamp to desk edge — no drilling required
  • Frees your entire desk surface instantly
  • AOC center · VA2247 flanking at 15–20° inward angle
  • ~$60–180 · biggest single desk transformation
🖥️

PC Placement

  • Floor left of desk on a caster riser — elevated 4–5" off floor
  • Glass side panel faces INTO the room — RGB visible from seated position
  • 6"+ clearance behind for exhaust — don't push flat against the wall
  • OMOTON caster stand ~$25 — pulls out easily for maintenance
  • Dark braided cable sleeve on PC power cable to surge protector
📡

Streaming Setup

  • OBS Studio on left monitor — always open when streaming
  • Webcam on top of center AOC — dragon shelf visible over your shoulder on camera
  • Boom arm mic at left desk edge — swings center when live, parks to side otherwise
  • Tricorn Black walls read clean and dark on camera — no distracting clutter
  • Your stream brand is already built: Dark Forest Dragon
🔌

Cable Management

  • Paintable cable raceway on west wall — paint Tricorn Black before mounting
  • USB hub velcroed under desk — one cable to PC, all peripherals into hub
  • Monitor cables behind arm post, velcro-tied every 12"
  • Wireless keyboard + mouse = two fewer cables entirely
  • Zero cables visible on desk surface — ADHD brain essential

Chapter 09 · All Rooms · Flame-Free Only

Scent Design

No flames. No burning smell. Ultrasonic diffusers use cold water mist — zero heat, zero smoke, the mist glows in your RGB lighting.

No Open Flames Anywhere In This Design

Given your seizure sensitivity: zero candles, zero incense, zero wax warmers. The eucalyptus shower bundle is safe — cold steam, not heat. Flameless LED candles produce no heat and no scent — they're fine for visual atmosphere only.

Starter Oils — Plant Therapy Brand

Cedarwood · Eucalyptus · Lavender · Peppermint · Sandalwood · Black Pepper. These six cover all five mood blends. ~$50–70 total, lasts months. Avoid MLM brands (doTERRA, Young Living) — good quality, dramatically overpriced.

Chapter 10 · Dragon Shelf

Collection + Sourcing

Books, gadgets, and dragons as one unified aesthetic. You're already a collector — you just need the framework to display it as a world.

You're the Scholar-Hoarder with Dragon Taste

Your existing books and gadgets ARE the collection. Dark-spined fantasy novels displayed spine-out under amber LED lighting IS dragon aesthetic. A beautiful gadget on a shelf IS dragon aesthetic. Dragon pieces accent a world that already exists from your interests. One intentional piece per month beats twelve impulse buys every time.

📚

Books as Display Objects

  • Dark-spined hardcovers spine-out — jewel tones glow under amber LED underlighting
  • Light-spined paperbacks turned inward or stored — they fight the dark palette
  • One book always forward-facing — your current read displayed like a small painting
  • Horizontal stack of 3–4 with a small object on top — creates depth on the shelf
  • Stormlight Archive, Cradle series, First Law trilogy — beautiful spine aesthetics
⚙️

Gadgets as Artifacts

  • Exposed-gear clocks, compass, astrolabe — navigation artifact energy
  • Metal dice set in a wooden box displayed open — $25–45 at local game store
  • Dark-finished tech: matte black charger, sculptural phone stand
  • Obsidian, labradorite crystal, ammonite fossil — natural curiosities from gem shows
  • Rule: if you use it AND it's beautiful, it earns shelf space
🐉

Dragons — Slow and Intentional

  • Material hierarchy: stone-look > metal > dark resin > plastic
  • Avoid: bright rainbow coloring, cute expressions, anything reading "gift shop souvenir"
  • Best sources: Design Toscano website, Etsy sculptors, McFarlane Toys (~$20–60)
  • The shrine piece — save for something you genuinely love, not the first you find
  • One per month maximum — the restraint IS the practice
📦

Three-Box Sort (Existing Stuff)

  • Display: Dark-spined books, interesting gadgets, dark crystals, DnD hardcovers
  • Store: Light-spined paperbacks, bright plastic, sentimental-but-off-aesthetic items
  • Release: Random trinkets you can't remember getting — let them go
  • Do this before anything touches a shelf. Takes one hour, saves months of visual noise

Chapter 12 · Twitch · She/Her

PNGtuber + Streaming

Dark forest dragon. Hoodie and horns. You, expressed through the aesthetic you've already built. Veadotube + OBS + your three-monitor setup = ready to go live.

The Character

Full human with dragon accents — dark fantasy hair (teal, jade, or ember red), gold slit-pupil eyes, dark curved horns, scattered scales on cheeks and collarbone, oversized dark hoodie with dragon emblem. She's you with dragon energy layered on. Same vibe, same aesthetic, same personality — just with horns.

🏷️

Name Suggestions

  • Vaelith — draconic, invented, clean handle, top pick
  • Soryth — shadow/ember root, short and memorable
  • Emberveil — dragon fire + forest mist, very on-brand
  • Draeven — dragon haven, fits the sanctuary vibe
  • Check Twitch + Twitter availability before deciding — short names win
🎭

8 Expression States

  • Idle — neutral, mouth closed, default when silent
  • Talking — same as idle, open mouth, mic-triggered
  • Happy — big smile, squinted eyes, gaming wins
  • Screaming — wide eyes, open mouth, gets clipped constantly
  • Smug — half-smile, raised brow, very on-dragon-brand
  • Annoyed — narrowed eyes, "this game is testing me"
  • Sad/Tired — drooped eyes, late night streams
  • Dragon Mode — glowing eyes, fangs, save for big moments
⚙️

Software — Veadotube Mini

  • Free at veado.tube — pay what you want
  • Mic-reactive mouth movement — talks when you talk
  • Bounce animation when speaking — gives the PNG life
  • Hotkey-triggered expression states
  • Add as Window Capture in OBS → Chroma Key out background
  • Position bottom-left during gameplay, centered for Just Chatting
📺

OBS Scene Setup

  • Gameplay: Game capture + PNGtuber overlay bottom-left
  • Just Chatting: Dark forest BG + PNGtuber centered large
  • Starting Soon: Animated overlay, no PNGtuber
  • BRB: Forest ambience loop
  • End Screen: Thank you + socials
  • Left monitor runs OBS · Right monitor runs Veadotube + chat
Art Technical Specs

Draw each expression as a separate PNG with transparent background. Minimum 1000×1000px, ideally 2000×2000px. Keep line art on a separate layer from shading — reuse the base drawing across states. Draw in order: Idle → Talking → Happy → Screaming. Launch with those four. Add the rest over your first month. Don't let perfectionism delay the first stream.

The Most Important Thing

Stream consistently even when the viewer count is zero. The first ten streams are practice, not your debut. The debut is whenever you decide it is. Start messy, start now, get better in public. Your aesthetic, your room, your personality — that's the content. The game is secondary.

Budget Strategy

$500–1,000 Phased Right

With Sunday days away and a tight budget, ruthless prioritization is the move. Sleep first, function second, aesthetic third. The room gets built in layers over weeks — not all at once.

🚨
Sunday Is Days Away

Do NOT try to do everything at once. The trap is spending $800 on Day 1 and having nothing left for things you actually need week 2. Spend $150–200 on Day 1 essentials, then phase everything else. The room will look sparse for a week — that's fine. It will look intentional within a month.

1
Day 1 — $150–200

Sleep setup, desk + PC functional, bathroom basics. You need to sleep, work, and not live out of boxes. Nothing else matters yet.

2
Week 1–2 — $150–300

Paint the bedroom and bathroom. Hang curtains. Get the RGB strips up. This is the highest-leverage spending — transforms the whole space before any furniture arrives.

3
Month 1+ — $200–500

Murphy bed, floating shelves, closet organization, loft furniture. These get phased in as budget allows. The room functions without them — they elevate it.

Sunday — Move-In Day

Day 1 Order of Operations

Do these in this exact order. The order matters — you don't want to set up the bed and then have to move it to set up the desk.

The Golden Rule of Move-In Day

Get the bedroom functional before anything else. You need to sleep tonight. Everything else — loft, bathroom decor, unpacking books — waits until you have a bed set up and the PC running. Do not get distracted arranging the loft at 3pm and then have nowhere to sleep at midnight.

1
First Thing — Walk the Space Empty
Walk every room before bringing anything in
Note outlet locations, light switches, where natural light hits. Takes 10 minutes, informs everything
Find the stud locations in your bedroom walls
Use your stud finder now while the walls are bare. Mark with painter's tape. You'll need this for curtain rod + shelves
Take photos of every room empty
You'll want these for reference, and for the before/after when it's done
Test all outlets, light switches, and fixtures
Find out now if anything doesn't work — tell the landlord immediately
2
Bring In — Bedroom First
Bring in your desk — place against the west wall
This is the anchor. Everything else in the bedroom positions relative to the desk
Set up the PC tower on the floor left of the desk
Connect monitors, keyboard, mouse. Get it booting before you do anything else — you'll need it tonight
Install curtain rod + blackout curtains on the west wall window
4–6" above frame, 6" past each side. Do this before dark — you'll sleep better
~$45–65 if ordered
Set up your temporary sleep situation
Until Murphy bed arrives: air mattress on the south wall, or mattress on the floor. Make it. Put clean bedding on it. Done
Connect to internet
WiFi or ethernet — get this running. You'll need it for everything that follows

The Morning After Rule

Monday morning — before you do anything else — make your bed properly with clean sheets. This one act makes the whole space feel settled and intentional even when there are still boxes everywhere. A made bed in a dark-painted room with RGB strips glowing is already the Dragon Den. Everything else is just adding to it.

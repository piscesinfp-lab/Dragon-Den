Zone Detail

Placement Specifications

Exact heights, distances, and hardware for every zone. Measure twice, staple once.

Zone 1

Peak Pendant

  • Hook: Matte black screw cup hook into center ridge beam — pre-drill 1/16" pilot hole
  • Drop length: 3–4 feet from beam — pendant should hang at ~5ft from floor
  • Cord routing: Along beam to nearest outlet, tacked with cord clips every 12"
  • Bulb: Smart Edison — Kasa or Govee, 2200–6500K range, dimmable
  • Style: Rattan cage or dark metal cage — $25–50 Amazon
  • Position: Center of room, directly under ridge beam peak
Zone 2

Beam Underlighting

  • Strip: Govee RGBIC 5m — cut to beam length, one strip per beam
  • Mount: Staple gun every 10–12" OR 3M VHB tape — both hold well on wood
  • Position: Center of beam underside, facing down into room
  • Controller: Tucked at one beam end, hidden behind beam edge
  • Power: Run cord along beam to nearest outlet — same cord path as pendant
  • Beams to cover: All 3 visible cross-beams — ~12–15ft strip per beam
Zone 3

Dragon Shelf RGB

  • Strip: Govee RGBIC — one strip per shelf, cut to shelf width (~36–40")
  • Position: Underside of shelf front edge, LED facing downward and outward
  • Heights: Shelf 1 at 28" · Shelf 2 at 44" · Shelf 3 at 60" from floor
  • Shrine spotlight: LED puck light screwed into underside of shelf above shrine piece — aimed down at 45°
  • Controller: Velcroed to back of bottom shelf, accessible but hidden
  • Power: Daisy-chain to a smart power strip on the east wall
Zone 4

Desk RGB

  • Monitor bias strip: Govee Immersion TV Backlight — camera clips to monitor top, strip runs behind all 3
  • Shelf strip: Govee RGBIC under floating desk shelf front edge — faces monitors
  • Bias strip height: Centered behind monitor cluster, 2–3" from wall
  • Camera position: Top center of main (AOC) monitor, angled slightly down
  • Power: USB-A into powered hub or directly into PC USB port
  • Screen sync: Govee app → PC mode → screen color reactive
Zone 5

Galaxy Projector

  • Unit: BlissLights Ark or Encalife Atmosphere — $35–55
  • Position: On desk shelf or small stand in desk corner, aimed at sloped ceiling
  • Angle: Tilted ~30–45° upward toward the A-frame slope — fills the angled ceiling with stars or forest canopy
  • Power: Smart plug — Kasa or Govee — scheduled on/off or voice controlled
  • Best effect: Run with beam underlighting off or very dim — projector is the star
  • Modes: Galaxy stars, aurora, or nebula — rotate with mood
Zone 6

PC Internals

  • Position: Tower on floor left of desk — glass side panel faces INTO the room
  • Software: iCUE (Corsair), Razer Synapse, or ARGB controller app
  • Sync: Match active mood color manually or via Govee API integration
  • Effect: Slow color flow or static match — avoid rapid strobe effects
  • Clearance: 6"+ from wall behind tower for exhaust — don't block RGB with cables
  • No extra cost — uses existing PC RGB hardware

Bathroom · 9'5" × 5'0"

Bathroom Lighting

Simple but transformative. Three changes turn the existing bathroom lighting from clinical into spa.

Primary Light

Vanity Bar — Swapped

  • Replace: Hollywood chrome bar with matte black bar fixture — same 4 screw holes
  • Bulbs: Warm 2700K ST19 Edison bulbs — 4 per bar
  • Effect: The Mineral Deposit walls glow deep teal-green under warm light — looks like an onsen
  • Dimmer: If outlet has a switch, replace with a dimmer switch — $12 at Home Depot
Accent

Mirror RGB Strip

  • Strip: Short Govee RGBIC strip (1–2m) behind or around medicine cabinet mirror
  • Default: Warm amber — makes the mirror glow like a vanity in a luxury hotel
  • Spa mode: Switch to deep teal or soft green — matches Mineral Deposit walls
  • Power: Plug into outlet beneath sink or beside mirror
  • Mount: 3M tape on mirror back — no drilling
Ambient

Flameless LED Candles

  • Units: 2–3 battery LED candles — counter and toilet tank ledge
  • Type: Warm flicker effect, remote controlled, timer function
  • Position: One on counter beside skincare tray, one on toilet tank
  • 100% safe — no flame, no heat, no burning smell whatsoever
  • These are the ONLY candle-style items in the whole design
Loft · 27'0" × 11'10"

Loft Lighting Map

Long narrow space with an A-frame peak and the triangle window at one end. Lighting divides the two zones while the string lights overhead unify everything.

DnD Zone

Table Pendant

  • Hook: Cup hook into A-frame beam directly above table center
  • Drop: 2.5–3ft from beam — pendant at ~5.5ft, clears heads when standing
  • Bulb: Edison warm 2200K, dimmable smart bulb
  • Effect: Pool of warm light over the table — everything outside it falls to shadow
  • Style: Cage pendant, rattan, or dark metal to match bedroom
Reading Zone

Arc Floor Lamp

  • Position: Behind and to the right of the reading armchair — arcs over the left shoulder
  • Bulb: Smart bulb — warm 2700K for reading, slightly brighter for tasks
  • No ceiling mounting needed — floor lamp, just plug in
  • Style: Matte black arc, matching hardware throughout
  • Creates a personal reading pool that doesn't bleed into the DnD zone
Canopy

Beam String Lights

  • Lights: Warm globe string lights or fairy lights — 15–20ft length
  • Route: Along one beam overhead, spanning from reading nook to above the DnD table
  • Mount: Small staple clips every 18" — minimal holes, removable
  • Power: Smart plug at nearest outlet — scheduled on at dusk, off at midnight
  • Effect: Unifies both zones under one warm canopy — the tavern ceiling
Feature

Triangle Window

  • No treatment needed — the window is the feature, not the lighting
  • At night, the dark forest outside + interior pendant glow creates natural contrast
  • Optional: a very small accent light aimed at the window frame from below — silhouettes the triangle shape
  • Never put curtains here — the view is the point
Bookshelf

Shelf Underlighting

  • Strip: Short Govee strip under the reading nook bookshelf top shelf
  • Default: Warm amber — books and decor glow
  • Subtle dragon presence: The one dragon figure on this shelf, lit from below, glows
  • Keeps the reading nook visually interesting without overpowering the pendant above the table
